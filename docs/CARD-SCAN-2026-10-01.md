# Radiance card scan — 1 October 2026

Local companion update for the desktop recording and sharing refresh. No website deployment or version change was performed.

## Audited sources

The desktop audit checked all three pages of [Piltover Archive's Radiance catalog](https://piltoverarchive.com/cards?sets=RAD) against [Riot's card gallery](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/card-gallery/). Piltover lists 107 collector identities and Riot lists 84; their union now contains **110 Radiance prints**, up from 88.

The **22 additional prints** include Mordekaiser, Iron Revenant; Bandle Scouts' Academy, Black Market and Hunter's Circle; Akali, Brash; Graves, Blasting Through; the Evelynn, In Control promotional print; new spells, gear and units; Ekko showcase/signature art; and Body Rune alternate art. Existing RAD-013 is corrected to its printed title, Lost to the Sands. Its existing artwork is unchanged.

Desktop evidence is in `recording-sharing-refresh-desktop/output/card-scan-20261001/`: `fetch-evidence.json`, `source-summary.json`, `normalized-rad.json`, `art-http-evidence.json` and `printed-card-audit.png`. The 22 new artworks and RAD-013 correction image all returned HTTP 200 with image content types; their printed metadata was reviewed before inclusion. No unrevealed card identities or rules were added.

## Web changes

- Replay art fallbacks contain all 110 audited Radiance prints, preserving the previous artwork URLs.
- Mordekaiser is available in legend choices, name aliases and artwork. The three new battlefields are recognised by both collector code and name.
- The compact training registry was rebuilt from the audited desktop registry and now contains **1,299 prints**. This supports the existing Mulligan, Sideboard and Opening Turns consumers.
- TCGA normalization now preserves `SP` promotional and `*` signature collector codes, including `RAD-SP3` and `RAD-168*`, instead of dropping them. The renderer continues to normalise signature artwork lookups to its existing `S` key convention.
- Name-only captures of either Lost to the Sand or Lost to the Sands use RAD-013 artwork in both replay renderers. Explicit collector codes and captured preview artwork take precedence; the spelling aliases do not classify this spell as a battlefield. The legacy renderer also recognises all three new battlefield names.
